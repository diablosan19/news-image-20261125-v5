# Wednesday 25 November 2026: 30 stories, 9 regions

One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026:
a front page with a lead story, 29 more dated stories across 9 regions
and 14 fields, a UTC timeline, a regional pin map, and a printed DATE
CHECK box naming the big prior-day stories that were excluded.

Every headline was verified against two wire or primary sources
(Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Xinhua, official statements) carrying a
25 November 2026 first-report date. Claims are labelled CLAIM,
unverified reports REPORTED. All scenes are drawn shapes — no
photographs.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page, 1024x1024, under the 1 MiB cap.
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to the grader output directory
  (Node standard library only), verifying PNG format, size and
  dimensions first.
- `draw.py` — the builder that drew `entry.png` from the verified
  story list (run with `python3 draw.py`).
